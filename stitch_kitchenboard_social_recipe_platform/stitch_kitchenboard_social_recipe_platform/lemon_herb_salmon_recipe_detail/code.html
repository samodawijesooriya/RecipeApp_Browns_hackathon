Lemon Herb Salmon

A bright, morning-fresh take on a weeknight classic. Ready in under 20 minutes.

timer Prep Time 10 min
skillet Cook Time 12 min
group Servings 2
bar_chart Difficulty Easy

What you'll need

  • 2 (6oz) Salmon fillets
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 Lemon, juiced & zested
  • 1 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • Salt & pepper to taste

The Process

1

Prep the fish

Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel. Season generously with salt and pepper on both sides. This ensures a good crust when searing.

2

Sear to perfection

Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Once shimmering, add the salmon skin-side up. Cook undisturbed for 4-5 minutes until a golden crust forms.

3

The flavor bomb

Flip the salmon. Add minced garlic, lemon juice, and zest to the pan. Baste the salmon with the juices for another 3-4 minutes until cooked through.

4

Garnish & Serve

Remove from heat. Sprinkle with fresh chopped dill. Serve immediately over roasted asparagus or a light quinoa salad.
